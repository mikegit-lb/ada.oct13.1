# Brief: "The Secret Map of the Magic Forest" (Present Continuous, NOW vs LATER)

Verbatim requirements from the requester, 2026-10-13. Topic slot filled with: Magic Forest.

## Role and learner
- Master ESL curriculum designer; gamified digital content for primary students (age 10).
- Output is interactive screen slides for an educational webpage (html, css, js, json, md).
- Learner: A1 English, native Turkish speaker.
- Tone: highly energetic, playful, extremely encouraging, visually descriptive.

## Grammar focus (critical): Present Continuous, two uses
- NOW (real action): what is happening right now.
- LATER (future plan): fixed, definite plans for tomorrow or soon.
- Core mechanism: the form (Subject + is/are + Verb-ing) is identical; context and the time marker (now vs later) decide the meaning.

## Story
- Interactive adventure "The Secret Map of [Topic]". Story moves naturally from current observation to future planning.

## Mandatory structure (4 stages)
- Format: distinct Markdown headings per stage; bullet points only; no narrative paragraphs; instructional/screen text.
- STAGE 1 THE HOOK & WARM-UP: engage curiosity, review basic vocabulary. Visual multiple choice, no grammar yet. Provide a simple image prompt.
- STAGE 2 CONTEXTUALIZATION, ACTION NOW (Observation Screen): present continuous for the present moment. Three visual prompts/slides of dynamic action. Drag-and-drop of the correct "be" form (is / are) into blanks, e.g. "The bird ___ flying."
- STAGE 3 CONTRAST & PLAN, ACTION LATER (Timeline Screen): present continuous for future arrangements, contrasted with Stage 2. Introduce time markers ("Tomorrow", "Next Week"). Use connecting phrases "We are going to..." or "Our plan is...". Sorting activity: two sets of images (Current/Future); student labels which set uses NOW vs LATER.
- STAGE 4 GAMIFIED ASSESSMENT (Challenge Screen): test both rules together. "Which Way Do You Go?" choose-your-path quiz, three unique scenarios. Per scenario: pick the correct time marker (NOW or LATER), then conjugate the verb correctly to pass through.
- Focus on teaching with great visuals and build many exercises to practise the topic.
