## STAGE 2: ACTION NOW (The Observation Screen)

- Goal: Use am / is / are + Verb-ing to say what is happening NOW.
- Interaction types:
  - Teach card
  - Drag and Drop (fill the blanks)
  - Build the Sentence (tap the word tiles)
  - Multiple Choice (tap the answer)
- Reward: map piece 2

### Screen 1: Teach: Look! It is happening NOW!

- Interaction type: Teach card (read and tap Next)
- Label: NOW = Şimdi
- Picture: Pip looks at animals moving in the forest
- Formula: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing
- Screen text:
  - We use am / is / are + Verb-ing.
  - The bird is flying now.
  - The frogs are swimming right now.
  - I am looking at the map.

### Screen 2: Drag and Drop

- Interaction type: Drag and Drop (fill the blanks)
- Prompt: Drag is or are into the blanks!
- Sentence:
  - The bird [is] flying.
  - The frogs [are] swimming.
- Chips: is, are
- Pictures: A bird flying and frogs swimming in the river
- Pip hints:
  - Hint 1: One bird? Then use is.
  - Hint 2: Many frogs? Then use are.
  - Hint 3: The bird is flying. The frogs are swimming.

### Screen 3: Drag and Drop

- Interaction type: Drag and Drop (fill the blanks)
- Prompt: Drag is or are into the blanks!
- Sentence:
  - The monkeys [are] eating.
  - The fox [is] running.
- Chips: is, are
- Pictures: Monkeys eating and a fox running
- Pip hints:
  - Hint 1: Many monkeys? Use are.
  - Hint 2: One fox? Use is.
  - Hint 3: The monkeys are eating. The fox is running.

### Screen 4: Drag and Drop

- Interaction type: Drag and Drop (fill the blanks)
- Prompt: Drag am, is or are into the blanks!
- Sentence:
  - I [am] looking at the map.
  - Pip and I [are] walking.
- Chips: is, are, am
- Pictures: Mia and Pip walking and looking at the map
- Pip hints:
  - Hint 1: I always goes with am.
  - Hint 2: Pip and I means two people. Use are.
  - Hint 3: I am looking. Pip and I are walking.

## STAGE 3: ACTION LATER (The Timeline Screen)

- Goal: Use the SAME form for plans LATER. Only the time word changes.
- Interaction types:
  - Story
  - Teach card
  - Drag and Drop (fill the blanks)
  - Sort (NOW or LATER bins)
  - Multiple Choice (tap the answer)
  - Build the Sentence (tap the word tiles)
- Reward: map piece 3

### Screen 1: Story

- Interaction type: Story (tap Next)
- Picture: The map shows a plan for tomorrow
- Pip says:
  - Wow! The map shows a plan!
  - It shows things for TOMORROW! 📅
  - Same form. New time word!

### Screen 2: Teach: Same form, different time word!

- Interaction type: Teach card (read and tap Next)
- Label: LATER = Sonra
- Picture: A sun and a clock for now
- Formula: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing
- Screen text:
  - Form: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing.
  - Only the time word changes.
  - now = Şimdi. tomorrow = Sonra.
- Compare:
  - NOW (Şimdi): We are climbing the hill now.
  - LATER (Sonra): We are climbing the hill tomorrow.

### Screen 3: Teach: Time words for LATER

- Interaction type: Teach card (read and tap Next)
- Picture: A calendar for later
- Screen text:
  - Tomorrow
  - Next week
  - We are going to visit the waterfall tomorrow.
  - Our plan is to visit the waterfall tomorrow.
- Türkçe İpucu:
  - Şimdi yüzüyorum.
  - Yarın yüzüyorum.

### Screen 4: Drag and Drop

- Interaction type: Drag and Drop (fill the blanks)
- Prompt: Which set is NOW? Which set is LATER? Drag the labels!
- Sentence:
  - Set A [NOW]
  - Set B [LATER]
- Chips: NOW, LATER
- Pictures: Set A: three scenes with a sun and a clock; Set B: three scenes with a calendar
- Pip hints:
  - Hint 1: Sun and clock? That means now.
  - Hint 2: Calendar means later.
  - Hint 3: Set A is NOW. Set B is LATER.

### Screen 5: Sort

- Interaction type: Sort (NOW or LATER bins)
- Prompt: Sort the sentences: NOW or LATER?
- Bins: NOW (Şimdi), LATER (Sonra)
- Cards:
  - Pip is swimming now. -> NOW
  - The birds are singing right now. -> NOW
  - We are eating lunch now. -> NOW
  - I am drawing the map now. -> NOW
  - We are visiting the waterfall tomorrow. -> LATER
  - Pip is meeting the owl next week. -> LATER
  - They are building a boat tomorrow. -> LATER
  - I am flying the kite next week. -> LATER
- Pip hints:
  - Hint 1: Look for now or right now.
  - Hint 2: Look for tomorrow or next week.
  - Hint 3: now = NOW. tomorrow or next week = LATER.

## STAGE 4: THE CHALLENGE (Which Way Do You Go?)

- Goal: Pick NOW or LATER, then build the right sentence at three gates.
- Interaction types:
  - Story
  - Which Way Do You Go? (choose the path, then build the sentence)
- Reward: map piece 4

### Screen 1: Story

- Interaction type: Story (tap Next)
- Picture: A forest path with three gates
- Pip says:
  - Three magic gates! 🚪
  - First pick the time way. Then build the sentence!
  - You can do it!

### Screen 2: Which Way Do You Go?

- Interaction type: Which Way Do You Go? (choose the path, then build the sentence)
- Gates:
  - Gate river: Listen! Right now, the monkeys ... in the river!
    - Path: NOW
    - Sentence: The monkeys are swimming in the river.
  - Gate hill: Tomorrow, Pip and I ... the Big Hill!
    - Path: LATER
    - Sentence: Pip and I are climbing the Big Hill.
  - Gate cave: Next week, Pip ... a magic boat!
    - Path: LATER
    - Sentence: Pip is making a magic boat.
- Pip hints:
  - Hint 1: Read the clue. Look for the time word.
  - Hint 2: Pick the right am / is / are.
  - Hint 3: Check the -ing spelling.
